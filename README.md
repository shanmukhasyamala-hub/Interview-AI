## 📌 Overview

InterviewVerse AI is a next-generation interview preparation platform that combines Generative AI, Computer Vision, Speech Analysis, and Resume Intelligence to create realistic interview simulations.

The platform acts as a personal AI Interview Coach by:

- Analyzing resumes
- Generating personalized interview questions
- Conducting AI-powered mock interviews
- Evaluating technical and communication skills
- Tracking confidence and body language
- Providing detailed feedback reports
- Generating personalized improvement roadmaps
